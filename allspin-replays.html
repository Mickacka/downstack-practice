All-Spin from replays

Load a TETR.IO replay of a player who uses all-spins: every spin they make becomes an exercise. You start from their board a few pieces before the spin, with the pieces they placed, and do the same spin. The replay is read on this device; nothing is uploaded.

Add a replay

Tetra League matches (.ttrm) and 40 Lines games (.ttr), from TETR.IO's current version. In TETR.IO, open a replay and download it, or export it from your recent games.
