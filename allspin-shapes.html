All-Spin shapes

The spin slots strong players use, from three TETR.IO games (a Tetra League match, a Quick Play and an Expert Quick Play game: 569 spins). What makes a spin is only what touches its slot and which of its columns are roofed: by that, each kind of spin comes down to a handful of shapes. Learn to see them, practise each one on its own, and watch the players use it.
