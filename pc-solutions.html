How you get there

Which solution? The decision path

The rules are about the order you place the pieces in, not the order they come: hold lets you play a piece later. Hold it, play the next ones, take it back when you need it (the piece left over can stay in hold).

    The solutions, in the order to learn them

    Each one is the answer for the bags the ones before it can't solve: its share of all bags, then the total with the ones before. The third bag comes with an empty hold and you place 6 of its 7 pieces. A piece drawn across a cleared row goes down after that row clears.

    Solutions computed on this site with its own rotation rules, over all 5040 orders of the third bag. The setups are from the Hard Drop wiki, which gives the same chances.
