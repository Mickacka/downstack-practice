All-spin setups

Each setup is a board and the queues that spin in it. Play a queue: every piece has to spin, its last move a rotation into a spot where it can't move left, right or up, and clear lines when it can. A piece in brackets only builds: it goes down anywhere that lets the next one spin. Pieces already drawn in the picture are part of the board, unless the queue's answer was drawn there.

· Peek draws where the next piece goes; Show Answer plays it out and writes the inputs (↓×n: soft drop n rows, ↓: to the floor).

From an all-spin catalog fumen. Every queue was checked on this site's rotation rules; a few alternatives that can't spin here were left out.
